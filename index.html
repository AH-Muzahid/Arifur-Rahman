About me

I am an aspiring urban planner and geospatial researcher pursuing an undergraduate degree in Urban and Regional Planning at Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET).

I focus on transportation planning, land-use planning, land readjustment, and climate-resilient urban development. I use spatial data, remote sensing, machine learning, and research methods to address complex environmental and urban challenges.

What i'm doing

  • Geospatial data science

    Spatial analysis for transportation, land use, land readjustment, and urban development.

  • Remote sensing

    Earth observation, Sentinel-1 SAR flood mapping, and Random Forest classification.

  • Urban planning

    Climate-resilient planning, urban mapping, cartographic design, and planning proposals.

  • Research methods

    Field research, statistical analysis, research writing, and data-driven reporting.
